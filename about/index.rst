About and Contact
*****************

About
=====

**EasyFleet** is an open-source, ROS 2 framework for orchestrating fleets
of multi-skill robots, developed by the Intelligent Robotics Lab at
Universidad Rey Juan Carlos. It provides the capability-discovery,
mission-control and fleet-wide navigation-coordination layer that sits on
top of a per-robot navigation stack such as
`EasyNavigation (EasyNav) <https://easynavigation.github.io/>`_.

We welcome contributions from the community, whether through issues, pull
requests, or ideas. If you or your organization are interested in
supporting the development or use of EasyFleet, feel free to contact the
project lead.

Contact
=======

If you are interested in EasyFleet, multi-robot coordination, or robotics
research in general, please feel free to reach out to the project lead.

We encourage filing issues and feature requests directly on
`GitHub <https://github.com/EasyNavigation/EasyFleet>`_. For questions
about usage or advanced configurations, GitHub Discussions or GitHub
Issues are the best place to start.

For inquiries about collaboration, consulting, or research partnerships,
you may contact the Intelligent Robotics Lab directly or the project
maintainer via GitHub.
